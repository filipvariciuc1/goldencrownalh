Our home

Care at the scale of a family, not a facility

Golden Crown Assisted Living is a residential long-term care provider in Glendale, Arizona. We are licensed for Directed Care, the highest level of residential care in Arizona, in a real house shared by no more than ten residents.

Small on purpose: with a handful of residents and caregivers on site 24 hours a day, help never has to travel down a long corridor. Meals are cooked in the kitchen, not delivered to it, and the people caring for your loved one know them by name from day one.

We are dedicated to providing a safe and comfortable home-like environment where our seniors can thrive and continue to live their best lives.

What we offer

Care shaped around each resident

A one size fits all approach does not apply to senior care. Everyone in our home is unique, so each resident's plan of care is personally crafted to suit their individual needs and preferences.

Assisted Living

Personalized support with daily tasks, bathing, dressing, and mobility, with as much independence as each resident can comfortably keep.

Directed Care

Arizona's highest residential care level: full support for residents who can no longer direct their own care, delivered gently and consistently.

Respite Care

Temporary stays that give family caregivers a real rest, knowing their loved one is safe, comfortable, and well looked after in the meantime.

Medication Management

Trained staff give every medication on time and in the correct dosage, keeping symptoms managed and families at ease.

Visiting Professionals

Physicians, nurses, physical therapists, and podiatrists come to the house, so residents rarely need to travel for care.

Transportation

Complimentary rides to medical appointments and outings, so life does not shrink to the size of the house.
